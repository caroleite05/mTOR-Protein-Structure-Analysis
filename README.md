# mTOR Protein Structure Analysis

This project focuses on the exploration and analysis of the structure and components of the **mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR)**, a serine/threonine protein kinase involved in the regulation of cell growth, metabolism, and other important cellular processes.

The project explores the structural organization of mTOR, its main domains, and the interactions between its structural components.

## Project Objectives

* Explore the three-dimensional structure of mTOR.
* Identify and characterize its main structural domains.
* Analyze the organization and function of its components.
* Visualize the protein structure using bioinformatics and molecular visualization tools.

<br>

## Tools

* Protein structure databases - [PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/)
* Molecular visualization software - [VMD](https://www.ks.uiuc.edu/Research/vmd/)
